## 120-Word Bio (English)
Advocate Md. Shah Alam is a Bangladesh Bar Council enrolled advocate practising in Dhaka and before the Supreme Court of Bangladesh. He handles criminal defence and bail petitions, land registration, mutation, Lis Pendens and partition disputes, family law and overseas divorce recognition, cheque dishonour (Section 138 NI Act), and corporate filings before RJSC.

A substantial part of his practice supports Non-Resident Bangladeshis (NRBs) living in the UK, United States, Canada, and the UAE with Power of Attorney attestation, property purchase and recovery, inheritance, and NID/passport legal matters.

The first online or phone consultation is free with no advance fee. Chamber fees apply only when visiting the Uttara or Judge Court chamber with documents or proceeding with formal representation.
